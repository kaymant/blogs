Variational Divergence Minimization

Hence, the divergence can be written in variational form:

Df(PX ∥ Pθ) = supT∈𝒯 EX∼PX[T(X)] − EX∼Pθ[f*(T(X))]

The maximizing value of t may depend on x. Therefore, instead of optimizing over a single scalar, we introduce a function T.

Let 𝒯 be a chosen class of such functions:

T ∈ 𝒯.

If 𝒯 contains the exact pointwise optimizer, equality is attained. More generally, restricting optimization to 𝒯 gives a variational lower bound.
